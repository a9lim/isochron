# People

The model is `src/engine/people.js` (`PEOPLE`; its header holds the forms), run on its own thread in the People scenario (`src/engine/people-worker.js`) and linked to the planet by `src/engine/peoplelink.js`. Headless: `tools/people-run.mjs` on a cached opening (`tools/people/`; `ENGINE=7` builds it the way the page's desktop engine sees it), `LIVE=1` beside a live planet (`tools/people/couple.mjs`); `tools/people/earth-score.mjs` scores today's Earth (below). Fig. 8's hyperbola: `src/engine/peoplefit.js`; Fig. 2's economy: `src/render/economy.js`. Tests: `tests/people.test.mjs`, `tests/economy.test.mjs`.

## Design

- **One loop, run four times** (Kremer's, standard, from memory): minds make ideas, ideas raise capacity, capacity makes minds. Each era changes what the minds are and what they draw on, and each transition is a bifurcation of the loop, not a scripted age.

  | Era | Minds | Capacity from | Loop |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Foragers | people | foraged NPP | closes only past a threshold in the connected population |
  | Farmers | people | cropland | Malthusian, hyperbolic |
  | Industry | people | fossil energy | broken by the demographic transition |
  | Compute | compute | electricity | closed again, hyperbolic, capped by energy and heat |

- **Few new sinks**: the people write through the planet's own inputs: carbon pulses, tillage in the erosion field, land use on the cover, waste heat and sulfate as forcings, the boiled sea.
- **Factors** split where a split brings its own dynamics: soil (regolith), forest (cover), fossil carbon and ores (finite, their return falling with draw-down), ideas (non-rival, forgotten), fixed capital, energy capital, compute. **AI is capital that does labour** (synthesis): a task CES, so tasks AI can't do bottleneck. **Soft forms over hard caps**: sums over uneven units harmonic, thresholds logistic hazards, floors exponential approaches.
- **Out of scope**: agents, named cultures, tech trees; polities and war until the roadmap's second batch (`roadmap.md`). What a superintelligence wants isn't modelled; the model says what each ending costs, not why it happens.

## The first people, spread and farming

- **The first people** (Homo sapiens; speculation) start at the heart of the broadest tree–grass mosaic (H.mosaic, b × 4·trees·grass, summed within `R_ORIGIN` 1,000 km) on the landmass that can hold the most of them (`seed`): people arise across an ecotone, and the best single cell is a near tie among tropical coasts. They begin with knowledge `Z0` (0.5, the Middle Stone Age's, the archaic peoples' before them), one population a cell. On Earth the run spans ~150 kyr to the dial. The archaic peoples aren't modelled; their mark is the home world-island's wary game (Large game, below). (The run had begun with the first hominins, 2 Myr out, as lineages: soft species with a heritable trait, splitting, merging and excluding one another. A trait that could differ between modern peoples explained nothing the model needed.)
- **The species' clock** (speculation): contact ℓ = ℓ_S(t) e^{z/`Z_L`} (`reachL`), ℓ_S rising from `L_S0` (1,000 km) toward `L_SMAX` (3,000) as a logistic, e-fold per `T_S` (35 kyr) at first: one clock for everyone. The tip is still the loop's threshold, crossed where density and climate let the minds within reach pass it (the Middle Stone Age's innovations that came and went with its people's numbers: Powell, Shennan and Thomas 2009, from memory); the people tip where their mean knowledge passes 1. The cap sits at the old trait's contact in the farming era (~2,900 km); capped at 4,000 the Americas held ~18% (their sparse minds, short of their bodies' totals, gain most from a longer reach). Foraging's yield is `F_SAP` (1.8) × the base density's, what the hominins' trait had come to by their tip.
- **Two domains of knowledge** (batch 1c, `roadmap.md`; synthesis): production (`zc`: techniques; every yield and its thresholds, the store and the seed, tillage's care, cold, boats and the open ocean, trade's and the cities' reach, the spread's dispersal, industry and the economy) and learning (`zl`: writing, numeracy, science; contact ℓ, copying's pace, voyages' pace, research's yield past industry). Each gains, forgets, copies (at learning's pace) and travels with migrants and voyages alike, and each has its own luck field. **Where ideas go** (a9; the size speculation): a place's new ideas are shared between the domains, learning's share ½ − `TILT` among those who feed themselves (foragers, herders, fishers, farmers: their thoughts go to their work) and ½ + `TILT` among city dwellers, fed by others (`TILT` 0.15), the cell's by its city dwellers' share of its minds; each domain gains twice its share, so the world's ideas are kept and an even split is one number's. Works keep learning (`W_WORK`) and not production (`W_WORK_P`, none: it's practised every day); trade carries learning at a neighbour's rate (`C_TRADE_L` 1: texts travel) and production at a tenth (`C_TRADE` 0.1: techniques are learned at someone's elbow). They're bound together only by **production's epistemic base** (speculation, after Mokyr's *Gifts of Athena*, 2002, from memory: techniques plateau unless the knowledge beneath them widens): production's ideas × sig((`D_BASE` − (z_P − z_L))/`W_BASE`) (1, 0.25), falling away once techniques run a unit ahead of learning; copying isn't bounded. Industry reads production alone, so learning reaches it through the base, contact and copying's pace (the bound alone: a need for both through a softer minimum of the two scored the same and was less parsimonious). **AI needs learning first** (a9; the split synthesis): its efficiency is algorithms' and hardware's, and algorithms are learning's, so AI's knowledge `za` is `ALG` (0.8) of learning's (the world's, weighted by people's research minds: AI is made where the researchers are) and the rest production's (the chips), by the log ratio of algorithms' progress (~3×/yr) to FLOP per dollar's (~1.35×/yr; Epoch, from memory); its capability and its labour a unit of compute read `za`, its robots' bodies production. On Earth's openings learning starts ~0.8 behind production at industry and closes by takeoff, so the takeoff's timing hardly moves (earthlike: 302 years after industry against 301); the split matters where learning lags, and in batch 2 where states race. **AI's research minds share their ideas as a city's do** (a9: labs are urban): learning's share of what they add is a city dweller's, ½ + `TILT`, whatever the cell's own mix. With an even split and their rates set alike the domains stay equal step for step (`tests/people.test.mjs`). On Earth production runs ahead from the first (the foragers' thoughts go to their work; the tip ~10 kyr sooner), learning ~0.9 behind at farming's start and the base slowing a tenth of the world's people, ~0.7 behind at industry, closing by takeoff as the cities grow. (Without where ideas go, the domains moved together, learning ~0.4 ahead everywhere and the base never binding; a farmer at half a forager's time for learning parted them too but penalized only farmers; learning's minds multiplied outside and in cities ran away once industry urbanized everyone, or slowed every era; a tilt of 0.3 held most of the farming world at the base and drew the farming era out to ~45 kyr.)
- **Minds' bodies** (synthesis): minds pool across land peopled at `MIND_REL` (0.3) of the inhabited world's mean density. A forager world is one body; once farming crowds the cores, the sparse margins part it, so a continent reached only through the Arctic or across a strait keeps Kremer's lag (the Americas, Australia). Knowledge is also copied from any neighbour who knows more, at a rate weighted by each side's density (`RHO_COPY`).
- **A region's luck** (speculation: the chanciness of discovery, which a mean field of ideas leaves out; `lucky`). One field for each domain, on shared bumps with amplitudes of their own (writing here, iron there). Past `Z_LUCK0` (2, a unit before farming's) a cell's ideas are × e^{σξ − σ²/2}, σ `SIG_LUCK` (0.4), ξ a smooth random field of unit variance (`K_LUCK`, 160, seeded bumps of width `L_LUCK`, 1,500 km, their amplitudes Ornstein–Uhlenbeck in knowledge, renewed e-fold per `Z_LUCK`, 0.3, of its domain's frontier's advance), its mean over the world's people one at every step. With ideas a mean field, knowledge across the connected Old World was flat to a few hundredths and its lead held at the densest minds from the first fields to the takeoff, so every new line of equal knowledge rose at one spot. Now the lead passes among the places where minds are thick. Luck also hastens history (the world follows its luckiest dense region, since copying spreads a lead faster than luck moves on): on Earth industry ~8–17 kyr after the first fields (~9–17 without), early takeoff ~235–275 years after industry (~270–390). It's gated past `Z_LUCK0` because on the foragers' tip too it had brought the tip early.
- **Crossing the sea** (synthesis, `tools/people/settle.mjs`): sea edges are every crossing of up to `HOPS` (6) sea cells, plus each landmass group's `K_LONG` (4) nearest groups to `LONG_HOPS` (40), then whatever is still apart. Then come ocean routes (`graph`): each landmass (by land alone) to every other within `OCEAN_HOPS` (80) cells, with up to `M_ROUTE` (6) landings at least `D_ROUTE` (1,500 km) from each other and from the crossings already made. Without them, Earth's Americas were joined to Eurasia only at Bering, and nothing crossed the Atlantic. An empty shore is settled by a voyage (`voyages`): `FOUNDERS` (50) at a rate `S_SEA` × crowding × e^{z/Z_V} × e^{−km/reach}. The reach is `L_SEA0` + `L_SEA` (1 − e^{−z/Z_SEA}) + `L_OCEAN` sig((z − `Z_OCEAN`)/`W_OCEAN`): rafts and boats along coasts and between islands, a few hundred km by the Upper Palaeolithic (Sahul, Bering), then deep-sea navigation in a few centuries about `Z_OCEAN` (6.2). Farming and industry spread across a crossing by e^{−km/reach} too. Cold opens faster with knowledge (`K_COLD`).
- **Crops' land** (`rates`; synthesis):
  - **Floodplains:** each river has a floodplain `W_FP` √Q km wide along its course through the cell (hydraulic geometry's width ∝ √Q, standard). There the crops are watered and the floods renew the soil (`FP_RENEW` of what the uplands lack). Only the growing season's warmth limits them, and they are cropped as often as it allows (GDD/`GDD_CROP`, at most `CROPS_MAX`: wet rice's two and three a year).
  - **Uplands:** rain-fed (Miami's rain limb). A crop's warmth is 1 − e^{−(GDD − GDD0)/GDD_E}: none in a boreal summer, two thirds at the prairies'.
  - **Fertility** is 1/(1 + leaching/(`L_FERT` (1 + erosion/`E_FERT`))), with leaching the drainage × e^{(T − 15)/T_LEACH}. Old, leached tropical soils lose their nutrients; young and eroding ones keep them (Walker and Syers, Porder and Vitousek, from memory). The uplands are cropped only in that share and lie fallow the rest (swidden), until industry's fertilizer makes it up.
  - **The arable share** is the floodplain plus the uplands a plough can work, at most `ARABLE_MAX`:
    - unfrozen;
    - flat enough (`S_ARABLE`);
    - with a growing period of `LGP_DRY`–`LGP_WET` months (FAO's length of growing period: warm months whose rain meets half the potential evaporation, standard), so a monsoon's wet months count where the year's mean aridity wouldn't;
    - not the cold grasslands' sod (`SOD`), which only industry's steel ploughs turn.
  - **Disease:** the warm, wet tropics' diseases thin farmers (`PATH`, malaria with clearing and standing water) until medicine.
  - **Fields** keep to each cell's deep, flat bottoms (`fieldSoil`; on stony, thin ground they still bear `Y_STONY`). Tillage strips the uplands' soil, `SDR` of it leaving the cell. Fields go back to the wild over `T_WILD` where no one works them, and nothing grows past the cover's heat limit.
- **Farming's origins and crops** (synthesis):
  - **Origins:** the hazard is `H_FARM` × domesticates × knowledge (z about `Z_FARM`) × settledness × steady harvests, × (1 − s)², where s is the suitability of the crops farmed here or next door. One climate's crops don't pre-empt another's own (rice and millet beside wheat), so a world has ten to thirty origins. Domesticates are the zones' (`DOMESTIC`: the seasonally dry climates' large-seeded annuals, next to none in the perhumid tropics) plus an oasis's: a river's floodplain in a dry land, `D_OASIS` × its floodplain share × aridity (after Childe, from memory). With deserts at none, the Nile's and the Tigris's floodplains could never begin farming, and Earth's first fields were by Lake Victoria and on the Congo's rim.
  - **Packages:** each carries its origin's climate, and its suitability elsewhere is a Gaussian in the coldest month, the warmest, log water (a floodplain's river counts) and the summer's share of rain (`CLIM_S`, after BIOCLIM's envelopes). Industry's breeding widens it (`BREED`).
  - **Spread:** a better-suited crop farmed next door is taken up at a rate by contact, the neighbour's fields and the gain (`C_PKG`). Once a crop is taken up, farmers breed it toward the place: s^a, with a falling e-fold per `T_ADAPT` (2 kyr) to `A_ADAPT`.
  - **Farmed share:** it rises toward the arable land × s/(s + `S_HALF`) × (1 + `S_HALF`), clearing empty land next to farmers too: the Nile's valley had no foragers to take crops up.
- **Demic spread** (synthesis):
  - **Migrants judge land by what they'd farm.** A farming people's migrants (their best knowledge past `Z_FARM`) see unfarmed land as fields: `MIGR_F` of its arable land, crops suited `MIGR_S`. That sets only the spread's weights, after Ammerman and Cavalli-Sforza's wave of advance (from memory). With the cell's own fields alone, a continent of foragers had looked full to them, and farmers never came.
  - **Industry's mobility:** railways and steamships add `D_IND` (10⁴ km²/yr, a logistic about `Z_IND`) to the dispersal, and the spread takes up to `NSUB_IND` (48) substeps a step once industry runs. Capped at `NSUB_MAX` (4), the economy's 10-year steps held migration to a forager's few km a year, and the Americas stayed empty past the takeoff on the page's planet.
  - **Industry's crops:** past industry, crops travel at its pace. The uptake of a better crop, and the fields' spread from next door, × 1 + (min(knowledge's pace, `IND_PACE`) − 1) sig((z − `Z_IND`)/0.5); the clearing at most `CLEAR_PACE` (5) times faster. At the Neolithic's ~1 km/yr, the settlers' crops had reached a few hundred km inland by the takeoff.

## Food, years, ages and works

- **The sea's people** (synthesis). A coastal cell's sea feeds foragers by its coast's export production (`sea`, the most of its sea neighbours' in `life.export`): `B_SEA` × x/(1 + x), x the production over `PS_REF` (13 g C m⁻² yr⁻¹, the coasts' median). It replaced a flat coastal bonus of half the land's foraging, and `B_SEA` (0.019) keeps the coasts' mean where that bonus was, so the sea's food moves toward upwelling coasts and river mouths: Earth's richest are the Canary Current's desert coast, the Yangtze's and the Congo's mouths. Fields don't displace it, it doesn't follow the land's rain or warmth (it steadies the foragers' harvest), and its fish are a stock (below). The first people's mosaic still reads the land's foraging with the old coastal factor, so their origin is unchanged.
- **Herders** (synthesis). Pastoralism on the land fields don't take, where people have animals (below) and knowledge passes `Z_HERD` (3.25, a quarter past farming's): `D_HERD` (0.8 a km² at `NPP_REF`'s grass) × the animals kept (their set × the herd's share × what the trypanosomes leave) × a soft minimum of the best summer pasture and the best winter one within a season's move (the cell and its neighbours), on the cell's own grass. Summer's is the grass's production; winter's what of it stands to be grazed, none where the coldest month is under −`T_SNOW` (8 °C) and the winter's four coldest months bring more than `P_SNOW` (60 mm) of snow. Productivity rises with knowledge to `CAP_H` (2). Herders take the unfarmed land where they'd feed more than foragers.
- **Large game** (synthesis; the overkill's timing speculation). A stock a cell, its share of what it would be without people: logistic at `R_MEGA` (3%/yr) less a take of `H_MEGA` × wariness × the foragers' density against `B0` (at most 3) × their foraging's knowledge (P_F/CAP_F), solved exactly; under `M_EXT` it's gone for good. Game on the home world-island (`home`: the origin's land, joined by land and by crossings of up to `D_ARCH`, 300 km, as cold as a knowledge of `Z_ARCH`, 1, holds: the archaic peoples' range, from memory) had long known hominins and is wary (`NAIVE_CO`, 0.15 of a naive fauna's take); beyond it game meets people naive and is hunted out. On Earth the island is Africa, most of southern and temperate Eurasia and half of Sunda; Sahul, the Americas, Madagascar and Siberia are naive. (It had been the land people reached before the first tip, which a start with the first people would make nearly the whole world.) Its meat is `MEAT` (30%) of foraging at the full stock, calibrated at `MG_REF` (0.6). Live, its share scales the cover's browsing and adds to its fire (`FUEL_MEGA`: the grass the herds would have eaten burns; `vegetation.js`, `disturbance`).
- **Herds** (synthesis on Diamond's argument, from memory: the large domesticates came from the large herbivores still standing where people had long lived; the values speculation; `tests/herds.test.mjs`).
  - **Animals:** a farming origin domesticates the large game about it (the most of its cell's and its land neighbours' `mega`, against `MG_REF`), so the home world-island's faunas give their farmers a full set and the ones beyond it, hunted out, give none. A package carries its origin's animals where it's taken up, and they pass by contact among people who know farming (and onto empty land beside them) toward the better-stocked neighbours' at `C_ANIM` (1%) a year by the edge's contact × the herds each neighbour keeps (its herd's share, what the trypanosomes leave, and its land's room for them, full at `K_KEEP` (30%) of the cell in fields or a good steppe's pasture, so the tundra, the deserts and the tsetse's belt pass them slowly), × knowledge's pace past industry (`anim`, 0–1 of a full set). Eurasia's and Africa's head start is then the overkill's, not set by continent. On Earth the Americas' origins have none; animals reach Alaska through the model's mild Chukotka a few thousand years into farming and hold about a third of a set across the Americas by industry, all of one soon after.
  - **The herd** is a stock, its share of what the grass and fodder could keep (`herdM`): logistic at `R_HERD` (15%/yr), from `A_SEED` (5%) when the animals arrive, less die-offs (`herdYears`), solved exactly; with people or without (domesticates go feral on empty grass). A year's die-off is `F_DIE` (1.5) × the pasture's shortfall past `TH_DIE` (0.15), its anomaly `E_RAIN` R + `B_DZUD` (0.1/K) × the winter's snowiness × T̄′ (a dry year's grass; a cold year where the winter is snowbound: the dzud), over the closure's spread or the realized years as the harvest's are. Under the closure a snowbound steppe's herd holds near nine tenths of what its land keeps (a year's T′ spread of 1 K); a realized dzud 4 K cold takes about a third of it, rebuilt over a decade or two.
  - **Uses:** herders' food is the herd's; mixed farming's animals (`animHere`: the set × the herd's share × 1 − `PATH_ANIM` (0.8) × the farmers' tropical disease, the trypanosomes' belt) manure the fields, making up `MANURE` (35%) of the fallow's lack, and pull the plough, lifting the organic ceiling's yield by `TRACT` (30%; industry's machines take over above it).
- **Fish** (synthesis). A coastal cell's fish are a stock alike at `R_FISH` (0.3/yr): taken at `H_FISH` where people live on the coast (by their crowding), and by industry's fleets at `H_FISH_IND` (0.45, past the regrowth) × its share, easing with knowledge as tillage does. Before industry they hold near 0.9; industry's fleets collapse them, and they come back as the take eases.
- **The planet's years in the harvest** (synthesis). The harvest's year-to-year anomaly is a = α R + β T̄′ over a year's log-rain anomaly and its mean temperature anomaly (`harvestCoef`): α = `E_RAIN` (× `IRR` by a river or lake), β the yield's warmth sensitivity d ln Y/dT, the crops' through their degree-days where warmth rather than rain limits them (at most `B_TMAX`, 0.5/K), the foragers' through Miami's temperature limb. Farmers bear it whole (its rain's part as the months their crops grow have it: the hungry season, below), foragers half less the sea's share. The closure (`harvestSigma`) takes the spreads the year keeps (`sigP`, and `sigTy`, a month's T′ spread × √ of its memory), correlated as the year makes them (a warm year a dry one over land). Where the dial resolves the year, the engine's `HarvestYears` (`peoplelink.js`, a hook on each of the year's steps) gathers each whole year's log-rain unit and T̄′ on the year's grid and sends their moments with `air`; the people pool them (`addYears`), and each step takes as many years as it lasts, the realized years' mean and spread standing in for that share of the closure (`demography`'s `real`). A drought decade or a volcanic winter is then a famine where it fell; outrun, the closure stands. The farming origin's steady-harvest term reads the farmers' full spread.
- **The hungry season** (standard in outline; the forms synthesis; `tests/hungry.test.mjs`). A harvest is kept until the next, so a single harvest's food must last the year and a warm delta's three crops only four months.
  - **The calendar** (`calendar`, per cell in `rates`): a month grows where it's warmer than 5 °C and, on the uplands, wet enough (the growing period's test); a colder month only waits (a winter wheat sown in autumn ripens in spring); a warm dry month ends the season. Each season of at least `M_SEASON` (2) growing months bears a crop each `M_CROP` (4) months, as many as the warmth allows (on the floodplain GDD/`GDD_CROP` to `CROPS_MAX`, watered whatever the rain; on the uplands at most two), each harvested at the end of its share of the season. Where the whole year grows and never cools past `T_ROOT` (18 °C), roots and plantains are dug as needed, a gap of `M_ROOT` (2 months; the perhumid tropics' yams, taro and bananas, from memory). The longest gap between harvests is the store's; the month before the harvest that ends it is the lean month (`H.lean`, −1 where none). On Earth: one harvest a year on the temperate uplands; on the tropics' floodplains and Egypt's a gap of ~0.35–0.5 of a year.
  - **The store's losses:** a harvest eaten evenly over a gap of G years, the store losing ℓ a year, keeps (1 − e^{−ℓG})/(ℓG) of itself (`kept`); ℓ is `L_KEEP` (30%) at farming's start, e-fold less per `Z_KEEP` (2) of knowledge past `Z_FARM`, to `L_KEEP_MIN` (2%), × `Q_ROT` (2) per 10 K of the year's mean past 15 °C (insects and moulds; pre-modern farm stores lost ~10–20% of their grain over a season, from memory). At farming's start a temperate single harvest keeps ~90% of itself, a tropical one ~74%, two tropical harvests ~86% and the tropics' roots ~95%.
  - **The harvest's spread:** its rain part is the crops' growing months' (white at the month), × κ = √(`M_CROP`/months grown), a single crop's as the year's was: several crops a year pool it (κ ~0.6–0.8 in the tropics), a season too short for one bears more.
  - **The reserve:** the store against bad years (`reserve`, `demography`'s store) is the granary's room past the season's need, `S_MAX` + 1 − G of a year as knowledge builds it (e-fold per `Z_STORE`): a single harvest keeps `S_MAX` (half a year) past its year, two harvests a year a whole year.
- **Age cohorts** (`src/engine/cohorts.js`; Siler's form and Lotka's stable population, standard; the riding synthesis). Each cell carries one-year cohorts to 100 that ride the total the demography solves: the total keeps its own law, the pyramid its shape, and the two agree on deaths.
  - Deaths past senescence, d_ns (hunger, disease and density, famine), fall on all ages as μ₀ (1 + `J` e^{−a/`T_JUV`}), μ₀ set so the cell's crude rate is d_ns (children most: ~40% dead by five in the pre-industrial reference). Senescence is Gompertz's, `S0` (1.2%/yr) at 60 doubling every ~7 years (`G` 0.1), × m^{`M_SEN`}: medicine m (1 before industry, `M_MIN` rich) lowers it by its square root, ~2× at the rich end.
  - A cell's aging, in its crude deaths, is m^{M_SEN} × its senescent rate past the pre-industrial reference's (births `B_PRE`, no growth: `reference`), never below none; the pyramid's own deaths are then the total's.
  - The rich end's stationary life table (e₀ ~80, 22% over 65, 1.8% over 90) is replacement: `B_MIN` (0.01245) is its crude birth rate (`replacement`, held by a test), so a rich world neither grows nor shrinks once its pyramid settles; after the transition it shrinks for a while as its large cohorts age. Without a pyramid the demography's closure (`AGE`) keeps the same endpoint.
  - Its workers' share is `LFP` × its working-age share (15–64) over `W_REF` (0.65), which the economy's labour sums.
  - All cells share one clock (`ageClock`), and cohorts move on whole as it passes each year (a boxcar), so steps of a month don't smear them; the step's integrals of the children's excess and senescence (at seventeen levels of medicine, lerped) are tables (`stepTables`). A step of `T_STABLE` (60 yr) or more leaves only its rates' stable population; those steps come before industry, so the pyramids are set from a table at the first shorter step.
  - Migrants move by a Rogers–Castro-like schedule (`MIG`: children with parents, a peak at 24), mixed in by mass (`settle`), so frontiers are young and the places they leave age. Births don't read the pyramid (a baby bust echoes in deaths and workers, not in births).
- **Cities' minds** (synthesis on urban scaling, innovation ∝ N^β: Bettencourt and others 2007, from memory). A city dweller's research for learning × (their city's size/`S_CITY_REF`)^{`BETA_CITY` − 1} (2 × 10⁴, 1.15; production's not), a cell's city dwellers its people × its `cityShare` (below); normalized over everyone, so the world's minds, and Kremer's calibration, are kept, and the loop runs faster where cities are large. `places` runs in the step every `DT_PLACES` (25) years once anyone farms.
- **Works** (speculation; `tests/works.test.mjs`). `S_WORK` (a quarter) of a city's people's labour a year builds works, in person-years. Their labour feeds no one: a city's capacity × (1 − `S_WORK` × its city share) before industry.
  - **Learning** (monuments, writing, schools): `F_LEARN` (half) of it, in the city, decaying over `T_WORK` (500 yr). Learning's forgetting falls as λ/(1 + w/`W_WORK`), w the learning's works a person (10 person-years halves it), so a people with works keeps what it knows through a crash.
  - **The land's works**: the rest goes back to the land that feeds the city, each place's by the share of the city's food it sends over its catchment, as far as industry hasn't taken over (× 1 − its share: railways, dams and machines are industry's capital). There it fills each kind by its gap to the room the place has for it (`workRoom`), each decaying over `T_WORK` unkept (canals over `T_CANAL`, 100 yr: they silt up), and what the land can't use goes to learning. Left alone they decay slowly, so an abandoned valley keeps a reason to be resettled.
    - **Terraces** (room `W_TERR`, 300 person-years a km², of the steep land a plough can't take where the rain would grow a crop: Andean and Asian terraces ~1,000 person-days a hectare, from memory) open that land to the fields (`arableOf`) and cut its tillage's erosion by `TERR_ERO` (80%).

Their fields are watered as the floodplain's (cropped as the warmth allows) on the uplands' soil, and salt.
    - **Granaries** (`W_GRAN`, 0.1 a person) raise the reserve against bad years × (1 + `G_X`) and cut the store's losses by `G_K`.
    - **Roads** (`W_ROAD`, 10 a km² of the land whose food goes to the cities) cut the cities' local routes' cost by 1/(1 + `R_X`), and with the canals the markets' routes by land (Trade, below).
  - **Salt** (standard in outline: irrigation in a dry land raises the water table and leaves the salts its evaporation concentrates; southern Mesopotamia's wheat gave way to barley and then fields were lost over a millennium or two, Jacobsen and Adams 1958, from memory; the rates synthesis). ## Routes, trade and cities

`src/engine/routes.js` (`ROUTES`), `tests/routes.test.mjs`; the cities in `People.places`, `foodshed` and `feed` (tests in `tests/people.test.mjs`).

- **The routes** (the roadmap's seam rule 2, one transport cost for every flow between cells; synthesis). A graph on the people's grid's parent (level 5, ~220 km), its nodes land or sea, each joined to its ring and to the six nodes between its ring and the next (`twelve`: twelve directions 30° apart, each new edge at the cheaper of its two two-step paths scaled to its straight length), so paths run within a few percent of the great circle. On the ring's six alone a path ran one straight leg and turned once (a hexagonal grid's metric is ~15% long off its axes), and flows sharing a first leg and parting at the turn drew triangles. Each directed edge has a cost in sea-km (a cargo carried a km by sea on a fair wind), on two layers:
  - **Sail.** By sea, a ship's year-mean speed along the edge (`sailSpeed`): through the water `V_SHIP` (2.5 m/s) before a fair wind of `W_REF` (7 m/s), a share of it by the angle off the wind down to a beat's `G_UP` close-hauled, at the year's six points of the planet's own surface wind, plus the gyres' current along the edge, never below an oar's `V_ROW`, over the open share of the year (sea ice closes it). Each leg sails when its wind serves, so where the wind reverses (a monsoon) both ways cost about twice a fair run, and where it doesn't (the trades) the way home is the long way round: the routes out and home differ, as the volta do mar's. By land, a road's `C_LAND` (30) a km × (1 + climb/`H_CLIMB`), a river both ends of which pass `Q_NAV` at `C_DOWN` (5) downstream and `C_UP` (10) up, a lake's `C_DOWN`, a port `C_PORT` (100). Sea : river : road ≈ 1 : 5 : 30 is the pre-industrial ratio (Diocletian's price edict of 301 as historians read it, from memory).
  - **Steam** (industry's): steamships at `C_STEAM` (0.3) a km, their speed `V_STEAM` with the current and `K_STEAM` of the wind along the edge (so the lanes gather where the sea helps, rather than scattering over the grid's equal paths), rail at `C_RAIL` (3) × (1 + climb/`H_RAIL`), barges at `C_BARGE`, ports at `C_PORT_IND`.
  - **Markets** are the grandparent grid's living land (level 4, ~450 km apart; ~800 on Earth), and any land the sail can't reach from one within `C_FILL` is given its own. Dijkstra from each, on both layers, gives every market's cost to every other, the paths and the sail's part by land (~2.4 s; built when anyone first farms, and again as the land changes under them, at most every `T_ROUTES`, 1,000 yr). On today's Earth, Lisbon to the Caribbean ~8,000 sea-km and back ~11,700; New York to Lisbon ~6,500 against ~10,600 out; Aden to Calicut ~7,800 and back ~6,000 on the monsoon.
- **Trade** (synthesis on the gravity model of trade, standard: Tinbergen 1962, from memory). Every `DT_TRADE` (10) years, between the markets that farm (trade comes with farming), a kernel e^{−c/reach}: c the round trip's mean on the layer the pair sails (steam by the more industrial end's share), the sail's part by land cheapened by the pair's works (÷ 1 + `R_ROAD` × their roads' built share + `K_CANAL` × their canals' share of the land, each the two markets' mean: roads at their room or canals over a fifth of the land halve it, the boats going where the water does; synthesis), the reach `R_T0` (300 sea-km) at farming's knowledge, ×e per `Z_RT` (1.1) past it at the more advanced end, at most `R_TMAX`. Grain by sea from Egypt to Rome then weighs ~0.3 of the local market's at Rome's knowledge, the Atlantic is within reach by the early modern era, and with steam the world is one market. Each market buys from the others in proportion to kernel × their food (its supply basket, `W`).
  - **The harvests pool along the routes.** A cell's harvest is (1 − τ) its own and τ its basket's, τ the share of food through markets (`TAU_AG` 15% with fields, toward `TAU_IND` 90% with industry). The weather's correlation between markets, ρ = e^{−d/`L_CORR`} (1,500 km), is taken as one factor (each market's anomaly its correlation with the buyer's, plus its own), so the basket's variance is Σ w²(1 − ρ²) + (Σ w ρ)² and its covariance with the cell's own Σ w ρ. The planet's realized years pool alike (`pooledYears`): a drought is eased by the basket's good years. Before industry the spread falls a few percent; after it, ~6-fold. This replaced a closure that pooled ~(ℓ/L_CORR)² harvests once industry came.
  - **Knowledge and crops travel the routes.** A cell copies the markets it meets by trade where they know more, learning at its neighbours' rate × τ (`C_TRADE_L` 1) and production at a tenth of it (`C_TRADE`), and takes up a better crop farmed by them (`C_TRADE_PKG`): the routes carried crops between farmers who never met (the Columbian exchange). Each market keeps the `K_PART` (12) it meets most, each met by its kernel × N/(N + `N_MEET`) (a town's 10⁵ people meet it at half), as much as a neighbour at most and all of them together at most one. (Ideas, one number then, at a whole neighbour's rate ran down the Americas' routes from the model's mild Chukotka, and they held ~24% of the world at takeoff on Earth, against ~19% without trade's ideas and ~21% at 0.3.) The people's own spread still walks their grid's edges.
- **Cities that must be fed** (synthesis). Each farming cell sends its cities a share of its food that follows its urban share (`U_AG` 8% once it farms, toward `U_IND` 80% with industry) over `T_URB` (40 yr: people move to the towns as fast as the work does, over about a generation; moved at once, the countryside starved faster than it could walk to the cities). Its own people keep the rest, and every cell gains what arrives (`feed`), its capacity and its migrants' alike (the food is the place's, whoever eats it), so a city holds more people than its fields feed, and the people follow the food by the spread.
  - **Where it goes** (`foodshed`, every `DT_PLACES`): 1 − `N_CITY` over the cell's catchment by the local routes (`Routes.catch`: its nearest `M_CATCH` cells by road, river and short crossing), each city drawing as its site (ports, rivers) × its density^`G_CITY` × e^{−cost/(`C_CITY0` + `K_CITY` reach)} (`C_CITY0` ~100 km by road whatever the knowledge: at the trade's reach alone, farming's largest city was a tenth of history's, ~25,000 at 65 million people); `N_CITY` (20%) to the `K_NATION` (48) largest cities over the markets' routes by their density^`G_NATION` × e^{−cost/(`C_NAT0` + `K_NAT` reach)} (`C_NAT0` ~500 km by road) (capitals and primate cities, Rome's grain from Egypt); reach the trade's at the cell's knowledge, the cost by sail or steam by industry's share. Of each, e^{−cost/(`K_LOSS` reach)} arrives: the carriers eat the rest.
  - A cell's **city share** (`cityShare`) is the share of its food that came in, its city's people its people × that share (`city`). A city's people die `D_CITY` (1%) a year past the countryside's (the crowd diseases: pre-industrial cities' deaths outran their births, kept up by migrants; standard, the size from memory), × the square of medicine's share still to come (sanitation made the rich world's cities as healthy as its countryside by ~1900, ahead of most of medicine; from memory).

## Peoples

Batch 1c's fourth landing (`src/engine/peoples.js`, `tests/peoples.test.mjs`; synthesis, the values speculation): who people are, beside what they know. Each cell's identity is a point on a torus of side `TOR` (8; a language apart is 1, a family ~2.3) for each of its ways of life (foragers, herders, fishers, farmers, city people), held by that way's people: Barth's groups that last side by side by living differently (1969, from memory). A continuum is points varying smoothly over the land, a mosaic is ways sharing a cell, and a people is only a region where points are close. The torus is Miasma's antigenic map ported (a9): drawn directly, one axis a hatch's angle and the other a hue, so the Culture map is a fill that turns and shifts with identity, and a boundary shows only where distant branches meet. Its cost is chance resemblance: in two dimensions unrelated peoples can drift into looking alike. (It replaced, the same day, two points a way of life in eight dimensions drawn as lines where the field tore: a line on nearly every cell edge, messy and fragmented, a9.)

- **Drift**: each point random-walks, `ID_SIG2` (2 × 10⁻⁴) a dimension a year, so two peoples parted drift apart as E d² = 4σ²t until the torus wraps: a language apart in ~1.25 kyr (Romance's), a family in ~6.5 kyr (Bantu's, Austronesian's; both from memory). Where migrants settle an empty place their points take a founder's kick (`ID_KICK`, 0.05 a dimension), so expansions split as they go (a serial founder effect). Drift is stepped exactly under the pull (an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck step: σ²(1 − e^{−2r dt})/2r a dimension); added whole after the pull, a 500-year step's walk had left neighbours a step's kick apart however strongly they were held.
- **Contact**: each point is pulled toward those its people meet, at `R_CONT` (1%/yr) × g(d), g = `G_FLOOR` + (1 − `G_FLOOR`)/(1 + (d/`D_CONT`)²) (0.05, 0.5): dialects level fast, unlike peoples converge slowly (a Sprachbund: borrowing, intermarriage) and never not at all, so neighbours settle a dialect apart and peoples a family apart only where contact thins (a sea, a desert, sparse land). (Without the floor, a pair once apart drifted apart for good and the foragers' 100 kyr shattered into isolates.) Its people meet their own cell's and their ring's, the ring's share 1 − e^{−(ℓ_id/dx)²} by its people and the land's ease (averaged over the land edges, a strait's added by e^{−km/ℓ_id}: averaged over all, a coast's many crossings had thinned its contact with the land beside it); between two ways of life at `X_WAY` (0.05: people meet and trade across them but seldom marry, Barth), whole between foragers and fishers (both forage) and with the cities, where ways meet. Identity's contact length ℓ_id is `ID_L0` (100 km: a band's marriages and a village's) until learning passes writing's `Z_ID0` (4), then e-fold per `Z_IDL` (1) to `ID_LMAX`: ~500 km at learning 6, ~1,400 at 7, the world by ~10 (writing's, print's and broadcast's reach; speculation; at e^{z_L/1.75} from the first, continents fused by farming). Past it the media pull every point toward the world's mean whatever the distance, `R_MEDIA` (2%/yr) × 1 − e^{−(ℓ_id/`L_WORLD`)²} (10,000 km): the world blends once learning spans it (a9). With drift, the nearest-neighbour pull is a stepping-stone field, whose identity distance grows as the log of the distance between places in two dimensions: Séguy's curve for dialects (1971; Nerbonne; from memory), tested on a lattice.
- **Ways of life** follow each cell's food (`capAt`: the land's, foraged or herded as herders hold it, the sea's, the fields', what energy buys) and its city's share. People change their way as the mix changes and keep who they are, but take `F_CONV` (a quarter) of the way to its customs: each non-urban way has a point on the torus (`att`, the four a half side apart from a seeded origin: cultural ecology, customs shaped by how people live; a9), and every point of that way drifts toward it at `R_ATT` (10⁻⁵/yr, ~100 kyr: weak, a9; at ~10 kyr it held the foragers' world within a unit of their point). Cities have no point of their own: their people's, mixed.
- **Migrants carry their points**, by way of life (the spread's fluxes past `ID_EPS`, 1%, of where they go a substep, and every voyage's founders), so farming's dispersals carry language families; fewer are absorbed by where they land.
- **Mixing**: people joining a way's point move it by a conformist mean, weights w^γ, γ = 1 + d²/(d² + `D_MIX`²) (0.3): near peoples average (people and mean identity kept), far ones shift toward the larger (Boyd and Richerson's conformist transmission, standard in form; three hundred taking in a hundred three apart move a tenth of the way, not a quarter).
- **Read back** (each soft): learning's copying from a neighbour × 1 − `ID_COPY` (1 − sim) as the learner's learning comes to texts (past `Z_ID0`), sim = 1/(1 + (d/`D_SIM`)²) (2, a family apart: the literate's language spans the spoken ones within a cultural sphere, Latin's, Arabic's, Literary Chinese's, Sanskrit's, Pollock's cosmopolis, from memory) between the two places' learned identities (a cell's city's point, else its largest way's); before writing, oral learning crosses languages by bilingual neighbours as production's tacit copying does. And a common language in trade's kernel (the markets' towns' identities): × (1 + `CL_TRADE` sim)/(1 + `CL_TRADE`) (0.5: strangers trade at two-thirds of a shared language's; gravity models find a common language lifts trade ~1.4–2×, from memory).
- **Stepped** every `DT_ID` (10) years at least (identity moves over generations; the takeoff's steps are days), each part exact over a long step.
- **On earthlike**: neighbours a dialect apart (median ~0.5 across an edge in the late forager era), a language apart on ~12% of edges and a family on under 1%; random peopled places ~1 apart (p90 ~1.8, some 40–80° of hue); the effective number of peoples (Leinster and Cobbold's similarity-sensitive Hill number of order 2, `count`, exact over the torus binned by `D_HILL`, 0.5, so the near-alike count partly as one and the largest weigh most) ~3–4 in the foragers' few great continua, ~12 as farming's dispersals carry their families out, falling to ~2 by the dial. The peoples cost ~8 s of a ~125 s run.
