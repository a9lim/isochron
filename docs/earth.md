# Today's Earth

`?seed=earth` opens on Earth's data instead of genesis's noise (`src/engine/earth.js`, `EARTH`; the data in `earth/`, made and scored by `tools/earth/`, whose README lists the sources, terms and commands; the credits are in `about.md`). `Planet` takes it as `opts.earth` (`openPlanet` loads it; the worker fetches it before building). Everything after the opening is the sim's own; it opens at half a year a second (`EARTH_DIAL`). The climate is tuned on it (the constants marked "Earth").

## The opening

- **The data** (`tools/earth/bake.py`), on the level-7 grid, each point in its nearest cell by area: the mean bedrock (ETOPO 2022), the mean grounded ice (the surface less the bed above flotation; shelves are sea), the lowest land (`gz`, where the cell's water runs), the sea's reach (the level at which today's ocean floods into the cell, from a flood of ETOPO's 60″ pixels: `gs`, a strait narrower than a cell), the share Seton et al. 2020 dates and its mean age, and each cell's plate. Coarser engines restrict it (the lowest land and the reach by their lowest nested cell), so levels 6 and 5 work from the one file.
- **The crust** (synthesis): the datum puts a new ridge `RIDGE` (2.5 km) below sea level, so the sim's subsidence gives Earth's ages GDH1's depths; each cell's thickness is solved so the sim's isostasy, with the trenches Müller's motions bend (`plates.md`, Trenches), stands it at its measured bed (within ~25–40 m rms; ~2,400 cells at level 7 held at `H_MIN`, where the bed is deeper than crust of its age could stand even bent, ~2,600 before the trenches). φ is one less the oceanic share; floor the age grid leaves blank deeper than `DEEP0`–`DEEP1` is aged by its depth to at most `AGE_DEEP` (100 Myr).
- **The ice** (`earthIce`): the grounded ice (29.3 M km³), the bed bent to its flexural balance, the crust solved again so the bed stands where measured; the water holds the sea at the datum.
- **The plates**: Müller et al. 2019's rigid plates today, its deforming networks for the cells those leave out (each to the bordering rigid plate that moves most like it, so Sundaland rides Sunda), microplates joined to the neighbour they share most boundary with that isn't across a subduction zone (so arcs ride their overriding plates), and plates across diffuse boundaries joined (`V_JOIN`, `V_DIFFUSE`: those moving within 0.5 cm/yr of each other all along their boundary, and those closing slower than 2 cm/yr across ocean): India, Arabia, Capricorn and Australia ride as one, as do NAM and SAM, and Eurasia with Sunda. 11 plates at level 6, each ω its motion over the last Myr in the absolute frame. Slabs hang where the motions close (`hangSlabs`), each as long as its closing keeps one in the sim (closing × `SLAB_TAU`, to `SLAB_MAX`), and under the Himalaya and the Zagros the continents' (`CONT_PULL`). No spin-up.
- **The mantle** (`plates.md`, The mantle): its graveyard from Müller's trenches over the last 200 Myr (`earth/slabs.json`, `slabs-4.bin`, baked by `tools/earth/slabs.py`), its piles under the South Pacific and southern Africa; the crust stands the measured bed less its dynamic topography.
- **The rest**: ten hotspots (Whittaker et al. 2015's compilation: Courtillot et al.'s seven primary plumes with Iceland, Galápagos and Yellowstone); the reversals (GTS2012 to 83.64 Ma, then the superchron, the seeded process beyond), so the stripes are Earth's anomalies; the orbit (`EARTH_CYCLES`, fitted to Berger and Loutre 1991, the present exact).
- **No hand-placed straits**: the baked reach joins the Mediterranean through Gibraltar (sill −341 m at 60″; its level-7 cell's mean −98 m), the Gulf through Hormuz (−52 m), the Red Sea through Bab-el-Mandeb and the Baltic through the Danish straits (−11 m), at levels 7 and 6 alike; the Black Sea (the Bosporus is narrower than 60″) and the Caspian are basins holding lakes; Panama is land.

## How it scores

Plates (`tools/earth/plates-score.mjs`, level 6): the opening's balance rms 4.80 cm/yr against Müller's 4.40, correlation 0.927 (0.83 before the diffuse boundaries were joined, 0.905 before the mantle's flow), over 20 Myr 0.96 → 0.85; the Pacific 8.3–9.3 cm/yr; Indo-Australia 3° off Müller's direction. Tibet stands 4.6–5.1 km for 50 Myr (its crust's median elevation, followed in its frames): India keeps closing on Asia (~0.5–1.4 cm/yr across the Himalayan front, Earth's ~4).

Climate (`tools/earth/climate-score.mjs`, level 6, settled; against UDel, OISST, GPCP and CERES EBAF):
- Land 3.8 K rms in the year, 5.6 in January, 4.8 in July; the sea's surface by latitude 1.1 K, its ice by latitude 0.04; land's rain 859 mm/yr (observed 718), log-ratio rms 0.71; Köppen classes 46% of land, groups 73%. Settled in 7 cycles. The rain by region against observed: eastern North America 850 mm (1,052; its arid share 9% against 0), eastern China 833 (1,008), India 635 (1,063), the Amazon 2,836 (2,375), the Congo 2,131 (1,627), East Africa 2,346 (917), the Sahel 1,137 (508), western Europe 719 (758). Tuned with `tune.mjs`'s `reg` (land's rain and six regions' beside `rad`).
- The top of the atmosphere: reflected 99.0 W/m² (CERES 99.0; clear sky 54.9, 53.3), OLR 241.3 (240.4; clear 268.1, 268.3), the clouds' forcing −44.2 and +26.7 (−45.7, +27.9); rms by cell 16.3 and 14.9 W/m², by 10° zone 3.6 and 5.6.
- The sea: the overturning 11.3 Sv (`EKMAN` and `LAMBDA` at Earth's measured scale), 1.2 PW north across 26° N; 60–70° N's sea ~2 °C (4.4 observed), the Nordic Seas ~2 against 6.3 °C, the Barents frozen (its water doesn't reach it).
- Doubled CO₂ warms it 2.8 K with the ice sheets held.
- Lakes: routed on each cell's lowest land, ~2.5 M km² at level 7 (the Black Sea to its spill, the Great Lakes, Baikal, Chad, Eyre); the Caspian too full (the Aral spills into it); Lake Victoria missing (no bathymetry under it). Their algae: the Caspian ~100 g C m⁻² yr⁻¹ (observed 50–110), the Great Lakes ~100 (50–250), Baikal ~70 (75–120).
- Tides on the page ~4.8 TW (Earth's ~3.7). People: the first hominins at the Sahel's wooded edge (~10° N, 28° E: the broadest tree–grass mosaic; East Africa itself is too wet and closed forest here). At early takeoff they score ~1.1 against where people live now (`tools/people/earth-score.mjs`, `people.md` "On Earth"): ~9–9.5 billion; Asia ~28% (58% now), its India and China ~3% and ~7%, under the climate's dry India and weak monsoon; Africa, the Americas and Australia too many, under the too-wet Sahel, East Africa and Australia.

## Known errors

- **Eastern North America**: its year ~5 K warm, January ~7 K, its rain under half Earth's. Its coast's sea is warm and open in winter (the gyres' boundary current runs up the coast with no cold Labrador Current inshore: the Gulf of St Lawrence ~9 °C in January against 0.5), and Canada is 8 K warm in January under the template's zonal subpolar low, so its winter high doesn't form.
- **Clouds**: deep convection's longwave about half Earth's (the Congo 33 W/m² against 54); eastern China's shortwave short (38 against 70); deserts too cloudy; the Pacific's cold tongue 2.7 K warm and convecting; the polar clear sky's OLR high (the linear A + BT misses the winter inversions).
- **Rain**: the deep tropics wet (Southeast Asia 3,958 mm against 2,162, the Congo and the Amazon by a fifth to a third) and the Sahel twice Earth's; India's monsoon short by two fifths (India's land peaks late, held to the sea's seasons); eastern Africa two and a half times Earth's: the Indian Ocean's water comes onshore on the template's easterlies (14 km³ a day across its coast at the long rains), where Earth's low-level flow runs along the coast (the Somali jet) and the column over the plateau is dry; the land's own evaporation is none of it (16%). Tibet and Greenland ~5 K cold.
- **The plates' drift**: Indo-Australia slows from 7 to ~4 cm/yr within 10 Myr (the Himalayan front's resistance and its continental keel), so the front closes at ~1 cm/yr against Earth's ~4. Africa is 32° off Müller's direction and North America 55° (the mantle's flow is a potential flow from two piles to the slabs: no toroidal part). Over 200 Myr the land's mean falls from 0.65 to ~0.3 km (`plates.md`, Hypsometry). The cells on the plate boundaries the straits sit on move by hundreds of metres to kilometres in the first Myr at level 7 (new crust and stacking); the straits' channels and their scour carry the seas through it.
- **The sea falls**, ~20 m in the first 2 Myr and ~45 in 3 (level 6): two thirds the ocean's basins deepening as 7 km crust replaces the opening's ~11.5 km mean and the floor ages, a third the ice moving from West Antarctica's marine sheet onto land in Alaska, Greenland and Arctic Canada (the same volume holds more water off the sea). The Gulf, ~35 m deep, drains with it.
