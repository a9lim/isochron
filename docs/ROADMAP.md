# Roadmap

## Next: the states' lives (batch 3)

Settled with a9 on 2026-11-24. States and classes still decide nothing: everything they do is a flux or a hazard. The whole is synthesis, its values speculation, its sources from memory until checked. It lands in parts, each built against its own oracles and watched on the states survey, none tuned: a landing need only run to an ending without a NaN or an absurdity, and the whole people's model is recalibrated once, after 3f (a9).

- **3a. Classes and secular cycles.**
  - *Classes by their claims.* Each way of life's people are commoners and an elite, the elite named by the asset whose return it lives on (a9's axis, orthogonal to the way): the herders' nobility on the herds' increase, the fishers' chiefs on rich, storable fisheries, the farmers' aristocracy on land rent. In urban places (cities, factories, datacenters) the elite is capital's: merchants on trade's margin, then the bourgeoisie on industry's profit, then the owners of compute and robots (a9). Foragers hold no storable surplus, so no elite. Per cell, an elite share a way; no second demography: the share moves by the two classes' own rates (`demography` read at each class's income, so the well-fed elite outbreed commoners before industry, Clark's survival of the richest, and with it their births fall first) and by mobility. Open: whether a colony's capital, invested from its state's pool, pays its profit to the colony's merchants or home (beside N_TAX's drain).
  - *Office* is a state's own elite (a9): a stock in the polity table, its positions what the state's take and tribute fund, spread across its places by where the take is gathered, the court at the capital; its claimants past its positions are its overproduction.
  - *The take.* The commoners' wage is their outside option, the best income the migration potential (3d; until then crowding's) reaches net of the route's cost, lowered by coercion: the elites' arms against the commoners' exit, π₀'s terms. The elite takes the rest of its way's output: Domar's free land, free peasants and idle landlords (1970), and Ricardo's rent rising as people fill the land, the cycle's first half. Famine falls on commoners: whoever holds the granary eats (Sen's entitlements).
  - *Stress.* Elites past their positions fall back over a generation or fight. ψ = the commoners' immiseration (their wage against its trailing mean) × the elites' overproduction × the state's fiscal strain (Goldstone's political stress; Turchin and Nefedov's secular cycles), a product of logistics. It speeds the core's loss of cohesion, D_ASA (1 + K_PSI ψ), and internal war kills and breaks works where no border moves and culls the elites, which resets the cycle. Where the dial resolves the year a bad harvest is a revolt where it falls, and a continent's run of them a general crisis (Parker's seventeenth century). The economy reads the classes only in 3f; here, the stress and famine's split.
  - *Instruments, first*: `tools/people/states-survey.mjs` over eight draws on Earth (state lifespans, the secular period, steppe empires and their peak area against the Mongols' ~24 M km², the share under unlike rulers by era, effective states at takeoff, the dates of decolonization's two waves), and a Histomap in the People tab (each state's share of the people over time, branching where it secedes).
  - *Tests, not targets*: wealth's Gini by way of life, ~0.25 foragers, ~0.42 pastoralists, ~0.48 farmers (Borgerhoff Mulder and others 2009); the Old World above the New after farming, its draught animals making land the scarce factor (Kohler and others 2017; the herds' traction is already in); pre-industrial extraction ~0.5–0.8 of the feasible (Milanovic, Lindert and Williamson 2011); a great state's median life 200–500 yr (~600 now); agrarian populations swinging over two to three centuries.
- **3b. Secession.** A region whose pull away passes its state's hold founds a successor at its largest city, taking its parent's shares there (a founding takes the free share). The hazard rises with the state's stress and the region's route distance and unlikeness to its rulers, against the state's power there. One rule for schism (a dynasty broken into its provinces) and both waves of decolonization: settler colonies, carrying their state but raised by their own frontier past the metropole's reach, break away while still alike (the Americas' 1776–1825); a schooled colony resisting strangers founds its state at once instead of going stateless (once schooled, a nation's people stand in for N_STATE's city). Successors inherit their parent's holdings in the region, not their nation's extent (uti possidetis), so partitions come where one colony holds two distant peoples. The border norm (a9): once the media span the world, T_WAR lengthens and conquest slows (Zacher's territorial-integrity norm; speculation as a mechanism). With the second wave it aims at ~14 effective states at takeoff (~8 now). Each polity keeps its parent.
- **3c. Capacity and tribute: war makes states, the steppe, vassals.**
  - *Fiscal capacity* κ, a state's: how much of the power it holds over a place it rules directly; its offices are office's positions. War pressure on its borders (the rivalry's parity) grows it toward a ceiling that rises with learning, and peace lets it decay (Tilly; Besley and Persson); the take it allows raises immiseration.
  - *Tribute* (a9: one flow): power beyond what a state administers becomes a flow of surplus to its capital, over the routes, from whoever does administer the place, by its power over the payer's core against the payer's own. A low-κ state is ringed by tributaries whose land elites keep their rent and pay up (vassals); war raises κ and rent becomes tax (centralization).
  - *The steppe.* Founding needs a fed city now, so a herders' steppe never has a state of its own. Herders found states where their arms gather beside settled wealth in reach, their capital a camp at the herders' centre of arms; herders deliver wholly to a herders' state, at DL_HERD to a settled one. With arms and no administration of farmers, a steppe state takes tribute or raids (goods and lives move, allegiance doesn't), growing with the agrarian state it milks (Barfield's outer frontier; Turchin's mirror empires). When stress breaks the settled frontier it conquers, its capital moving into the largest city taken: a dynasty with the steppe's cohesion to spend (Ibn Khaldun's cycle with its actors). Gunpowder closes the steppe, M_HERD fading past a production threshold (Perdue). Deserts' herders by the same rules; sea raiders perhaps (speculation).
  - *Conquest elites* (a9). A state's identity becomes its office's own point on the torus (seam rule 1 lists identity in the polity table): set at founding from its capital's people's, kept when its capital moves, and drawn toward its capital's people as they are drawn toward it (R_STATE). Each reader of a state's identity reads it in place of its capital's people's (`capSlot` and `ids.sim(c, q.cap)`: kin's welcome, the schooled's resistance to strangers, the state's and the media's pull), so a steppe dynasty in a conquered city rules as strangers until it assimilates; now it becomes the city's people at once. A conquest replacing a province's aristocracy (the Normans) needs elite points a way and a cell, for later.
- **3d. Migration: one potential.** People move down the gradient of log real income a person: food a person before industry, which is crowding N/K (today's rule, by construction), and goods with it after. Across the sea between settled coasts at the route's cost, as now; cut across borders (B_MIG, tightening as nations school: the 1920s quotas); the emigration hump, by the young adults' share from the pyramids (Hatton and Williamson); chain migration toward one's own people, × (1 + K_CHAIN likeness): diasporas, a city's enclaves on the Culture map; refugees, W_DEATH split into deaths and flight from war (flight carrying identity, not allegiance); settlers carrying their state as now, so mass migration makes settler colonies for 3b's first wave. 3a's wages read its outside option.
- **3e. Heat by place.** The world's hE (its warming over T_HAB) becomes each place's own, from its hottest months' wet-bulb temperature (Stull's formula, from T12 and a humidity field the people's fields gain from the climate's moisture). Outdoor labour falls with it (Dunne and others 2013); heat's deaths by the hottest month's excess and the year's spread, falling with income; past ~35 °C (Sherwood and Huber 2010; ~31 in Vecellio and others 2022) the land can't be lived on outdoors, and 3d does the rest. Cooling is bought with energy as food is (W_FOOD), by cooling degree-days, so warming raises energy's demand. A check, not a target: the people by mean temperature against the human climate niche (Xu and others 2020). The boiled sea stays the world's.
- **3f. The economy reads the classes, then the recalibration.** Cities by the elites' spending (Cantillon's landlords) in place of U_AG; saving by class (classical, after Kaldor) in place of SAVE (K/Y ~2.3 against Earth's 3–4); ideas split by class as well as by place (Mokyr's upper tail; Squicciarini and Voigtländer), industry's spark reading the elite's learning last, since it's a lever on a bimodal onset. Coordination (a9; last): elites as a factor, Y = A E^μ L^{1−μ}, μ by what there is to organize, near none for herders and fishers, more for fields with works, most for cities and industry (Lucas's span of control), so the right elite share is μ, below it labour is under-organized (war's toll on works gains an organizational half), and an elite's income is a functional μY beside an extractive rent. With AI, compute and robots are capital, so their owners take what AI's work earns: the class the race's claims read (2c). The industrial top decile's U (Piketty, the WID) and the world's Gini, ~0.5 in 1820 to ~0.65 late in the twentieth century (Bourguignon and Morrisson 2002), join the targets; then the people's model is recalibrated whole.
- **Drawn**: estates as tesserae sharing one furrow angle (a holding's breadth, from the farmers' elite share and its rent), smallholdings a patchwork of angles; nomad camps marked apart from capitals; the top decile's share and inequality by way of life in the People tab; Fig. 2's people split into classes, with rent, wages, taxes, tribute and profit as arrows.
- **Deferred to crowd diseases** (a9): the forced migration that followed the Americas' emptying, its precondition, which the model lacks. If built: a coerced flux along the sea routes from where states' wars take captives to colonies rich in land and short of people, carrying identity and not allegiance, with the passage's deaths and abolition, drawn only as the identity it carries, and the about panel saying what it was in plain words.

## Open in the people's eras (batches 2a and 2b)

Landed on main on 2026-11-23 (docs/MODEL.md has the forms): states, overseas empires and regional economies. Where they stand, as medians of eight draws on Earth after the refit to the climate's first round (MODEL.md's table), open for batch 3's recalibration:

Past 6 billion the people grow ~0.8%/yr to the dial against history's ~1.1, and births falling later or medicine's floor lift them only with the whole industrial curve.
- **The industrial world consolidates** to ~8 effective states at takeoff, the largest with ~26% (history ~14): industry's arms favour the large and rich, and decolonization's new states are few (3b's second wave and border norm aim at it).
- Goods' trade has no term of its own (borders cut food's trade and ideas' copying, which carry it); ores set where industry begins but pay no rent.

## Parked: the race (batch 2c)

Its detail is the race's own, so it may be built with the game (batch 4), whose last act is the race (a9). Its seam is ready: the endings' draw reads a state (seam rule 4).

1. **The race.** Each polity has its compute, research minds and algorithmic frontier: grown by research, bounded by its learning, leaking across borders at a rate that closure lowers, with export controls on chips. The endings' draw reads the leader's margin in years, the contenders within a year, and the share they spend on safety and coordination (likeness on the torus, trade's ties). Alignment's odds rise with the margin and with safety (Armstrong, Bostrom and Shulman's race to the precipice); a pause's odds rise with coordination. The draw's mechanics stay (seam rule 4).
2. **Claims on the machines** (speculation, said so on the page): the aligned endings ask "aligned to whom", and claims by polity decide who returns and who is housed.

Left out: industrial war's nuclear exchanges (soot into the climate's forcing), a catastrophe outside the AI endings, to come after the race.

## Then: the game (batch 4)

- A third scenario beside World and People, the only one with controls; the other two keep the dial alone.
- The frame follows Scott Alexander's "The Goddess of Everything Else" (2015; none of its text is used). The model is the drive to multiply and consume, and the player never opposes it, only bends it. The score is the effective number of kinds the drive hasn't flattened: a Hill number over peoples, crops, polities, cities, works, worlds and stars. A star the probes reach could seed the next run.
- Standing rules: the player composes rules from words that are objects in the world (after Baba Is You). A rule pairs a trigger on a quantity with an action on a flow, then runs unattended. Revisions are scarce. Rules are ideas: each spreads by copying and takes root, held by a polity or its works, before it acts. Their forms open with the eras: prophecies, commandments, edicts, constitutions, manifestoes.
- The race as the last act: the takeoff's century played among the polities, each choosing pace against care. A treaty costs its signers.
- Needs saves (snapshots of the engine's and the people's workers) and, for rewinds, a repeatable GPU plate step (below).

Ideas not yet placed: place names laid down in layers; crowd diseases and the meeting of long-parted worlds (Miasma's SEIR; batch 3's deferred forced migration with them); ruins the planet buries and subducts; a census; one cohort's life at the slow dial; an ancestry traced back by the coalescent; the Silurian epilogue (the planet run on after the ending); a second rise on a planet whose coal is spent; worlds unlike this one (spin, tilt, no Moon, tidal locking).

## Open in the planet

- **The climate's next round: moist static energy.** The largest error on land is the dry subtropics' summers, 4–7 K cold (the Sahara's July 25 °C against 32.5, the Middle East's 24 against 30, the US Southwest's 15 against 21), with deserts' clear sky ~35 W/m² darker than CERES's to keep them from being colder still (`ALB_LAND`'s note). The energy balance diffuses temperature, so it imports heat only where land is colder than its neighbours; Earth's atmosphere moves moist static energy, c_p T + L q, down its gradient, so dry air draws heat in, and the Sahara is a radiative sink of ~23 W/m² and the hottest land (standard in zonal models: Frierson and others' moist EBMs, from memory; for deserts in 2D, synthesis). Tried on a scratch copy (2026-11-23): diffusing h with the moist part implicit on each edge, D(1 + μₑ)∇T, μ = (L/c_p) RH ∂q_s/∂T, the rest lagged, brought the deserts' summers within ~1–2 K (the Middle East's July 30.3, the Sahara's 32.2), but as a swap it scored worse (the tuner's objective 45.5 against 40.7 after ten rounds over ten constants): every continent imported heat and warmed (+2.6 K), because a land humidity of 0.2 + 0.6β reads even humid land drier than the sea; the template rained on the heat (the Sahara ~1,000 mm) until the ITCZ was placed at the sector's largest h, and then India's monsoon failed (its pre-monsoon soil dry, its h low); and it didn't settle within 16 cycles, the soil's wetness, the humidity and the heat feeding back between cycles. Needed: a land humidity that knows advected moisture (neither β nor the moisture's column humidity, which the rain scheme holds at ~0.4 over land and ~0.6 at sea, serves), the ITCZ and the rain's thresholds on h (heat lows stay on T: the Saharan heat low is real), the settling, the GPU's per-level edge weights rewritten each step (the lagged part can ride `XF`), a guard for hot climates (q_s past ~60 °C), and a full retune; the people's eras checked after. A go/no-go: a CPU prototype that settles and gets India and the Sahara both.
- **Eastern North America**: its year ~4.5 K warm (its January ~6.6) and its rain ~0.8 of Earth's; Canada's January ~8 K warm. - **The Nordic Seas and the subpolar oceans**: the Nordic Seas ~5 K cold (1.3 °C against 6.3) and the Barents' sea ice ~70% of the year against ~30; the subpolar Atlantic and Pacific at 40–55° N 2–4 K warm.
