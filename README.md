# Isochron

An Earth-like planet on one globe, from plate tectonics down to weather, with every layer coupled to the others.

The first phase is the tectonic globe. Plates move under their own slab pull and ridge push. Seafloor is made at the ridges and recorded in magnetic stripes, it sinks at the trenches, and continents collide, weld and rift. Sea level is solved from the water the basins hold. Climate, ice, the carbon cycle, the oceans and the weather follow on the same globe; see `docs/PLAN.md`.

Part of [a9l.im](https://a9l.im). Vanilla ES modules with no dependencies; the globe renders in WebGPU.
