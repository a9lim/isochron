# Isochron

An Earth-like planet on one globe, from plate tectonics down to weather and people, with every layer coupled to the others. A logarithmic dial runs it from hours to ten million years a second, and `?seed=earth` opens on today's Earth.

Live at [a9l.im/isochron](https://a9l.im/isochron/); `about.md` is its about panel. It is part of the [a9l.im](https://a9l.im) repository: serve the parent's root with `node tools/sims/serve.mjs` and open `/isochron/`. Vanilla ES modules with no dependencies; the globe renders in WebGPU. `AGENTS.md` covers the code, the tests and the tools.
