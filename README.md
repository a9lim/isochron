# Isochron

An Earth-like planet on one globe, from plate tectonics down to weather, with every layer coupled to the others.

Plates move under their own slab pull and ridge push; seafloor is made at the ridges and recorded in magnetic stripes, sinks at the trenches, and continents collide, weld and rift. Rivers wear the mountains down and build shelves. A seasonal energy balance, a template of the winds and a moisture balance make the climate and its soft Köppen zones; ice sheets grow and melt with the orbit's cycles; a carbon thermostat balances volcanoes against weathering; wind-driven gyres warm western coasts; and a two-layer weather model draws a synoptic chart with fronts and dry lines. A logarithmic dial runs it all from 2.8 hours to 10 million years a second. See `docs/PLAN.md` and `AGENTS.md`.

Part of [a9l.im](https://a9l.im). Vanilla ES modules with no dependencies; the globe renders in WebGPU.
