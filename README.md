# Isochron

An Earth-like planet on one globe, from plate tectonics down to weather, with every layer coupled to the others.

Plates move under their own slab pull and ridge push; seafloor is made at the ridges and recorded in magnetic stripes, sinks at the trenches, and continents collide, weld and rift. Rivers wear the mountains down and fill lakes. An energy balance with clouds, a template of the winds and a moisture balance make the climate and its soft Köppen zones; the sea and the air carry heat; ice sheets grow and melt with the orbit's cycles; a carbon thermostat balances volcanoes against weathering, through plants, the sea's carbon and dust; tides slow the spin; and a two-layer weather model draws a synoptic chart. People can be set on it, from the first hominins to the machines that outgrow them. `?seed=earth` opens on today's Earth. A logarithmic dial runs it from hours to 10 million years a second. See `AGENTS.md` and `docs/`.
